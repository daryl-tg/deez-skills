## Sub-features

- `dt-place` a tool places a drawing on the chart.
- `dt-toolbar` the floating toolbar appears for the selected drawing.
- `dt-delete` the selected drawing is removed.
- `dt-colour` the floating toolbar's colour popover keeps a typed colour on an
  outside click and drops it on `Escape`.
- `dt-cancel` `Escape` during a creation leaves no drawing behind.
- `dt-context` a right-click on a drawing opens the app's menu for it (settings,
  clone, copy, lock, hide, order, show on, delete) above the chart's own rows.
- `dt-templates` the floating toolbar's templates button lists the drawing's
  saved looks, saves one, makes the current look the default for new drawings
  or resets it; `Escape` closes one layer at a time.
- `dt-settings` the floating toolbar's gear opens the drawing's settings card,
  whose menus (line style, text position) are ComboBoxes.
- `dt-alert` a flat drawing offers an alert from its bell and its right-click
  menu; a sloped one does not while the one-click flag is dark.
- `dt-parallel-levels` a Parallel Line's settings card lists its levels.
- `dt-frvp` a Fixed Range Volume Profile's settings offer cluster, ladder and
  no-row display modes, Imbalance, Value Text, a range candle, a box border and
  background, and range stats.
- `dt-multi` with several drawings selected, the right-click menu, the
  floating toolbar and the gear act on all of them (source only).
- `dt-chart-menu` a right-click on the plot opens the chart menu, led by a card
  with the price under the pointer, a copy button and an alert button.
- `dt-cursor-memory` the cursor group's pick (Crosshair or Arrow) holds across
  a reload: the chart engine starts on the remembered cursor (`f17e7dd90c`).
- `dt-position-bar` a selected Long or Short Position gets a one-pill bar:
  Risk, Settings, More and Remove, with Lock and Templates inside More; a
  signed-in trader also gets an order button and an alert.
- `dt-picker-settle` a picker tool (Emoji, Icon, Image) closed with nothing
  picked is put down, and the toolbar returns to the cursor.
- `dt-magnifier-chip` on a phone, a `Magnifier` switch sits beside the `Draw
  by holding` pen while a tool with a lens is armed (`d3537e5ac1`; phone
  only, source).
- `dt-search` the tool search: `;` or the magnifier opens a small panel
  beside the toolbar; typing ranks the tools, `Return` or a click arms the
  pick, and four toolbar switches (Magnet, Stay in drawing mode, Lock, Hide)
  can be flipped from it (`9a0ab7a3cf`).
- `dt-levels-ink` a Fibonacci, Gann or pitchfork drawing takes one colour:
  Auto, Up, Down or a picked colour, on the floating bar and in the settings
  card, with `Color each level` and the built-in `Classic` template for the
  old per-level look (`8ebbd28367`, `694c096f0c`).

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Click a tool in the left toolbar, then click two points on the chart.
- Press `;` (or click the magnifier at the top of the toolbar, or type a
  letter with the pointer over the toolbar), type a tool's name and press
  `Return` to arm it.
- Select a drawing to get its floating toolbar. `Delete` removes it.
- In the floating toolbar, click the `+` swatch to pick a custom colour or type
  a hex; click away to keep it, press `Escape` to drop it.
