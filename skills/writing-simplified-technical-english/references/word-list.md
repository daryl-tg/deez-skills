# Word substitutions for agent documents

These entries come from the ASD-STE100 Issue 9 dictionary and its list of
recurring errors. They cover the words that occur in agent instruction
documents. This is a working subset, not the dictionary. The full dictionary
has about 900 approved words, and ASD keeps the copyright. Get the full
document at https://www.asd-ste100.org/ when exact approved wording is
necessary.

An approved word is in capitals. A word that the dictionary does not approve is
in lower case.

## Modal verbs and prohibitions

| Not approved | Approved |
|---|---|
| shall, should | MUST (v) |
| may (permission or ability) | CAN (v) |
| have to | An action verb in the imperative form |
| never | DO NOT |

Rule 3.4 removes a compound verb, but uncertainty has priority. Keep "the gate
can fail" when the source does not state a failure.

## How to use this list

1. Replace the word when the approved word gives the same meaning.
2. When no replacement gives the same meaning, write the sentence again with a
   different construction (Rule 9.1).
3. When the word is a technical noun or a technical verb of the repository,
   keep it (Rules 1.5, 1.6, 1.12). `commit`, `merge`, `push`, `rebase`,
   `worktree`, and `prompt` are examples.
4. Use one word for one action through the whole document (Rules 1.11, 9.4).
